S'mores cookies

Ingredients Steps

Ingredients

  • 1.5 cups All-Purpose Flour
  • 1 tsp Cornstarch
  • 0.5 tsp Baking Powder
  • 0.5 tsp Baking Soda
  • 0.25 tsp Salt
  • 10 tbsp nsalted room temperature butter
  • 0.5 cup packed light brown sugar
  • 0.25 cup granulated white sugar
  • 1 large egg
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • 4 sheets roughly chopped graham crackers
  • 0.75 cup semisweet chocolate chips
  • 1 bar broken Hershey's Chocolate bar
  • 1 cup Mini Marshmallows

Steps

To make these s'mores cookies, preheat your oven to 350°F (175°C) and whisk your dry ingredients together. Separately, beat the softened butter and sugars until fluffy, then mix in the egg and vanilla before blending the wet and dry ingredients into a soft dough. Fold in the chopped graham crackers, chocolate chips, and half the marshmallows, scoop into balls, and press the remaining marshmallows and Hershey's shards onto the tops. Bake for 9 to 11 minutes until the edges are golden, then let them cool on the pan for 5 minutes.
