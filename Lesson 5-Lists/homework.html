The Best Chocolate Chip Cookies

Lately, I've been craving chocolate chip cookies, especially the ones from Levain. Chocolate chip cookies are also something that is really fun but really easy and simple to bake. It's also a hit every single time no matter what! Everyone will always enjoy your cookies, leaving you happy and satisfied fromt the positive feedback and your delicious cookies.

Ingredients you need:

  • 1 cup of softened unsalted butter
  • 3/4 cup granulated white sugar
  • 3/4 cup packed light brown sugar
  • 2 large eggs
  • 2 teaspoons pure vanilla extract
  • 2 1/4 all-purpose flour
  • 1 teaspoon baking soda
  • 1 teaspoon salt
  • However many chocolate chips you would like!

Steps to follow

  1. Preheat and prep
    • Heat your oven to 350˚F
    • Line a large baking sheet with parchment paper
  2. Mix dry ingredients
    • Whisk the flour, baking soda, and salt together in a medium bowl.
  3. Mix butter and sugars
    • In a large bowl, mix the softened butter, granulated sugar, and brown sugar together until smooth and creamy. (This should take about 2 minutes)
  4. Add wet ingredients
    • Add the eggs and vanilla extract to the butter mixture
    • Mix until fully combined
  5. Combine wet and dry
    • Slowly mix the dry flour mixture into the wet ingredients just until a dough forms.
    • Do not overmix
  6. Add chocolate
    • Gently stir in the chocolate chips with a spatula
  7. Scoop and bake
    • Drop rounded tablespoons of dough onto the baking sheet, leaving some space in between each cookie
    • Bake for 9 to 11 minutes until the edges are golden brown and the centers are still a bit soft.
  8. Cool
    • Let the cookies cool on the baking sheet for 5 minutes before moving them to a wire rack to cool completely

Extra Notes

  • Make sure to have a wooden spoon or a rubber spatula
  • Make sure your butter is softened to room temperature
  • Stop mixing as soon as you see the last streaks of flour disappear
  • It's okay to underbake it slightly. Cookies are better when the outside is crispy and the inside is still a bit soft
  • Add as many chocolate chips as you'd like! That's completely up to you!
